## colum information
**elective_surgery**
Whether the patient was admitted to the hospital for an elective surgical operation,0 or 1

**ethnicity**
The common national or cultural tradition which the person belongs to

Caucasian,African American

**gender**
Sex of the patient,M or F

**apache_3j_bodysystem**

Admission diagnosis group for APACHE III,

Cardiovascular,Sepsis,Neurological

**icu_admit_source**

The location of the patient prior to being admitted to the unit

Accident & Emergency,Operating Room / Recovery,Floor
